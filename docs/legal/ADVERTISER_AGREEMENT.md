# Advertiser Agreement

**Big Starz Casting Platform — Advertising Terms**

**Effective Date:** Upon Signature

---

This Advertiser Agreement ("Agreement") governs the placement of advertisements on the Big Starz Platform.

---

## 1. Ad Placement Terms

### 1.1 Ad Inventory
The Platform offers the following advertising placements:
- **Banner Ads:** Header, sidebar, and footer placements
- **Sponsored Listings:** Featured casting calls and talent profiles
- **Video Ads:** Pre-roll and mid-roll on AI-generated content
- **Newsletter Sponsorship:** Placement in Platform email communications
- **Push Notifications:** Targeted mobile notifications (with user consent)

### 1.2 Targeting
Advertisers may target ads based on:
- User role (talent, casting director, creator)
- Geographic location
- Subscription tier
- Content categories (film, TV, theater, etc.)
- **No targeting based on sensitive characteristics (race, health, etc.)**

### 1.3 Ad Approval
All ads must be approved by the Platform before placement:
- Standard review: 24-48 hours
- Expedited review: 4 hours (additional fee)
- Ads may be rejected for violating content guidelines

---

## 2. Content Requirements

### 2.1 Prohibited Content
Ads must NOT contain:
- False or misleading claims
- Adult content or sexually explicit material
- Hate speech or discriminatory content
- Illegal products or services
- Malware or deceptive links
- Content that infringes intellectual property rights

### 2.2 Industry-Specific Rules
- Casting-related ads must comply with all Casting Director Agreement rules
- Talent agency ads must disclose fees and commission structures
- Equipment/service ads must represent actual products
- Educational program ads must be accredited where required

### 2.3 Disclosure
- Sponsored content must be clearly labeled as "Sponsored" or "Ad"
- Native ads must include disclosure before user interaction
- Affiliate links must be disclosed

---

## 3. Payment Terms

### 3.1 Pricing Models
- **CPM (Cost Per Mille):** $5-$15 per 1,000 impressions
- **CPC (Cost Per Click):** $1-$5 per click
- **CPA (Cost Per Action):** $10-$50 per conversion
- **Flat Rate:** Custom pricing for premium placements

### 3.2 Minimum Spend
- Minimum campaign budget: $500
- Minimum daily budget: $20

### 3.3 Billing
- Payment required before campaign launch
- Invoicing available for campaigns over $5,000
- Net-30 terms for approved advertisers
- All fees subject to applicable taxes

### 3.4 Refunds
- Unused budget refundable if campaign is canceled before start
- No refunds for completed or in-progress campaigns
- Disputed clicks subject to fraud review

---

## 4. Performance Metrics

### 4.1 Reporting
Advertisers receive access to:
- Impression and click-through data
- Conversion tracking
- Audience demographics (aggregated, anonymized)
- A/B testing tools

### 4.2 Metric Verification
- Platform metrics are considered official
- Third-party tracking allowed with Platform pixels/tags
- Discrepancies over 10% subject to investigation

### 4.3 Performance Guarantees
- Minimum CTR guarantee for premium placements
- Make-goods for under-delivery
- No performance guarantees for standard placements

---

## 5. Termination

### 5.1 Campaign Cancellation
Advertisers may cancel campaigns with:
- 48 hours notice for standard campaigns
- 7 days notice for flat-rate/pre-negotiated campaigns

### 5.2 Termination by Platform
The Platform may terminate ad campaigns for:
- Violation of content requirements
- Non-payment
- User complaints
- Technical issues

### 5.3 Post-Termination
- Active ads removed within 24 hours
- Performance data available for 90 days
- Outstanding invoices remain due
