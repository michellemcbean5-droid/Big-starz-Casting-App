# AI Consent Agreement

**Big Starz Casting Platform**

**Effective Date:** Upon Signature

---

This AI Consent Agreement ("Agreement") governs your consent to the use of artificial intelligence ("AI") technologies on the Big Starz Platform. By signing this Agreement, you provide explicit, informed consent for the AI processing activities described below.

---

## 1. Explicit Consent for AI Processing

### 1.1 Consent Grant
You hereby provide explicit, informed, and voluntary consent for Big Starz Inc. ("Platform") to process your personal data, likeness, voice, and other provided materials using artificial intelligence technologies.

### 1.2 Understanding of AI Processing
You acknowledge that you understand:
- AI processing involves automated analysis, generation, and transformation of data
- Results may vary and are not guaranteed to be accurate or desirable
- AI-generated content may not perfectly represent your likeness or intentions
- The Platform uses third-party AI models (HuggingFace) subject to their own terms

### 1.3 Scope of AI Processing
Your consent covers the following AI processing activities:
- Text generation (scripts, scenes, descriptions)
- Image generation and manipulation (headshots, scenes, composites)
- Video generation (reels, music videos, scenes)
- Speech processing (text-to-speech, speech-to-text)
- Face analysis and recognition (for digital twins)
- Text embedding and semantic analysis (for matching)

## 2. Digital Twin Creation Consent

### 2.1 What is a Digital Twin?
A digital twin is an AI-generated digital representation of your likeness, created from photographs, videos, and voice samples you provide. It can be used to generate new content featuring your likeness.

### 2.2 Consent for Digital Twin
You specifically consent to:
- The creation of a digital twin from your provided materials
- The storage and maintenance of your digital twin on Platform servers
- The use of your digital twin for content generation as specified in this Agreement
- The potential improvement of the digital twin over time with additional data

### 2.3 Digital Twin Requirements
You understand that:
- A minimum of 4 high-quality photographs is required
- Video clips improve digital twin quality but are optional
- Digital twin creation is only available to Silver and Gold subscribers
- You may update or regenerate your digital twin at any time

## 3. AI-Generated Content Rights

### 3.1 Content Ownership
- You retain ownership of your original photographs, videos, and voice samples
- The Platform retains ownership of the AI generation technology
- AI-generated content is licensed as specified in the [Likeness Rights Agreement](LIKENESS_RIGHTS_AGREEMENT.md)

### 3.2 Usage Rights
You grant the Platform the right to:
- Generate content using your digital twin and provided materials
- Display AI-generated content on your profile
- Use AI-generated content for Platform improvement and training (with separate opt-in)
- Watermark all AI-generated content

### 3.3 Your Usage Rights
You may:
- Download and use AI-generated content for personal portfolio purposes
- Share watermarked AI-generated content on social media
- Use AI-generated content for audition submissions
- Request deletion of specific AI-generated content

### 3.4 Prohibited Uses
You may NOT:
- Remove or alter AI watermarks
- Represent AI-generated content as authentic, unmodified footage
- Use AI-generated content for deceptive or fraudulent purposes
- Resell or commercially license AI-generated content without Platform approval

## 4. Revocation Process

### 4.1 Right to Revoke
You have the right to revoke your AI consent at any time, subject to the limitations below.

### 4.2 How to Revoke
To revoke AI consent:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Privacy > AI Consent**
2. Click "Revoke AI Consent"
3. Confirm your decision
4. Optionally provide a reason for revocation

### 4.3 Effects of Revocation
Upon revocation:
- AI generation access is immediately disabled
- No new AI processing of your data will occur
- Your digital twin is scheduled for deletion within 30 days
- Existing AI-generated content may remain on the Platform per the Terms of Service
- Your subscription tier may be adjusted if AI features were the primary reason for your tier

### 4.4 Limitations on Revocation
Revocation does not affect:
- AI-generated content created before revocation
- Content that has already been distributed or downloaded
- Data used in aggregated, anonymized analytics
- Legal obligations to retain certain records

### 4.5 Reinstating Consent
You may reinstate AI consent at any time by repeating the consent process. A new digital twin will need to be created.

## 5. Data Usage for AI Training

### 5.1 Opt-In for Training
AI model training using your data requires a separate, explicit opt-in beyond this general AI consent.

### 5.2 Training Data Usage
If you opt in to AI training:
- Your anonymized data may be used to improve AI models
- Your data will be aggregated with other users' data
- Personal identifiers will be removed before use in training
- You may withdraw this opt-in at any time without affecting other AI features

### 5.3 No Sale of Training Data
We do not sell your personal data to third parties for AI training purposes.

### 6.1 Safety Filters
All AI generation is subject to automated safety filters that:
- Detect and block harmful, illegal, or inappropriate content
- Prevent generation of non-consensual intimate imagery
- Block content that violates community guidelines

### 6.2 Human Review

- First-time generation for new users

If you encounter inappropriate AI-generated content, report it immediately through the Platform's reporting tools or by contacting safety@bigstarz.com.
