# Casting Director Agreement

**Big Starz Casting Platform — Casting Director Terms**

**Effective Date:** Upon Signature

---

This Casting Director Agreement ("Agreement") governs your use of the Big Starz Platform as a Casting Director. By signing this Agreement, you agree to the terms and conditions set forth below.

---

## 1. Platform Usage Terms

### 1.1 Account Type
As a Casting Director, your account provides:
- Ability to post and manage casting calls
- Access to talent profiles and search tools
- Application review and management tools
- Direct messaging with talent
- Analytics on casting call performance

### 1.2 Verification
Casting Director accounts require verification of:
- Professional identity (IMDb, company website, or industry references)
- Project legitimacy (for high-budget postings)
- Compliance with applicable labor laws

### 1.3 Subscription Requirements
Posting casting calls requires an active subscription:
- **Free Directors:** Up to 3 active casting calls, basic search
- **Bronze Directors:** Up to 10 active calls, advanced search
- **Silver Directors:** Unlimited calls, featured postings
- **Gold Directors:** Unlimited calls, priority support, analytics

---

## 2. Casting Call Posting Rules

### 2.1 Accurate Information
All casting calls must include:
- Accurate project title and description
- True compensation details
- Actual shoot dates and locations
- Legitimate contact information
- Realistic requirements

### 2.2 Prohibited Content
Casting calls must NOT:
- Request nudity or sexual content without proper disclosure and compensation
- Discriminate based on protected characteristics (except where bona fide occupational qualification applies)
- Advertise unpaid work as "exposure only" for professional-level roles
- Include false or misleading information
- Promote scams, pyramid schemes, or illegal activities

### 2.3 Compensation Transparency
- All professional roles must specify compensation
- "Deferred payment" must include realistic timeline
- "Copy/credit/meals" only acceptable for student/independent projects with full disclosure
- Commission-based roles must disclose commission structure

### 2.4 Minor Safety
- Casting calls for minors must comply with child labor laws
- Guardian consent is required before any minor participation
- Work permits and studio teacher requirements must be met
- No overnight shoots for minors without guardian approval

---

## 3. Talent Contact Terms

### 3.1 Respectful Communication
All communication with talent must be:
- Professional and respectful
- Related to legitimate casting opportunities
- Free from harassment or discrimination
- Compliant with applicable anti-harassment laws

### 3.2 Privacy
You agree to:
- Use talent contact information only for casting-related purposes
- Not share talent information with third parties without consent
- Not add talent to marketing lists without opt-in
- Secure talent data appropriately

### 3.3 Response Obligations
- Acknowledge applications within 7 business days
- Provide status updates on application review
- Notify selected and non-selected talent of final decisions
- Maintain professional courtesy at all times

---

## 4. Fee Structure

### 4.1 Posting Fees
- Standard casting call: Free (with subscription)
- Featured/promoted listing: $25 per week
- Urgent casting (24-hour turnaround): $50

### 4.2 Success Fees
- For castings resulting in paid talent engagement: 10% of first compensation payment
- Waived for talent who apply through Platform but are hired independently
- No fee for unpaid/student projects

### 4.3 Payment Terms
- Subscription fees billed monthly
- Success fees invoiced within 30 days of talent start date
- All fees subject to applicable taxes

---

## 5. Content Guidelines

### 5.1 Project Verification
High-budget projects (>$100K) require:
- Proof of funding or production company
- Valid production insurance
- Compliance with SAG-AFTRA agreements (if applicable)

### 5.2 Location Requirements
- All shoot locations must be safe and legal
- Proper permits must be obtained
- Insurance must cover talent while on set
- Union requirements (meal breaks, turnaround) must be met

### 5.3 Age-Appropriate Content
- Content ratings must be accurately described
- Adult content requires age verification of all talent
- Explicit content must be clearly labeled before application

---

## 6. Termination

### 6.1 Termination by Director
You may terminate this Agreement at any time by deleting your account or contacting support.

### 6.2 Termination by Platform
The Platform may terminate or suspend your account for:
- Violation of posting rules
- Fraudulent casting calls
- Harassment of talent
- Failure to pay fees
- Violation of Terms of Service

### 6.3 Effect of Termination
- Active casting calls will be archived
- Outstanding fees remain due
- Talent contact information must be deleted (unless legally required to retain)
