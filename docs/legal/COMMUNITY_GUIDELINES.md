# Community Guidelines

**Big Starz Casting Platform**

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

**Last Updated:** June 15, 2024

---

## 1. Our Commitment

Big Starz is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and professional community for talent, casting directors, creators, and industry professionals. These Community Guidelines ("Guidelines") set the standards for behavior on our Platform.

By using the Platform, you agree to abide by these Guidelines in addition to our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) and [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

---

## 2. Respectful Conduct

### 2.1 Treat Others with Respect
All interactions on the Platform must be respectful and professional:
- **Be constructive** — Provide feedback that helps others improve
- **Be courteous** — Use polite language in all communications
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome diversity of backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives
- **Be professional** — Maintain industry-appropriate standards

### 2.2 No Harassment
Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited, including:
- Bullying, intimidation, or threats
- Unwanted sexual advances or comments
- Discriminatory remarks based on race, gender, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, or other protected characteristics
- Doxxing or sharing private information about others
- Stalking or repeated unwanted contact

### 2.3 No Hate Speech
The Platform does not tolerate hate speech, including:
- Content that promotes violence against individuals or groups
- Content that attacks people based on protected characteristics
- Symbols or imagery associated with hate groups
- Content that dehumanizes or mocks individuals or groups

---

## 3. Content Standards

### 3.1 Accurate Representation
All content must be truthful and accurate:
- Profiles must represent your actual skills, experience, and appearance
- Casting calls must describe real opportunities with accurate details
- AI-generated content must not be misrepresented as authentic footage
- Credits and credentials must be verifiable

### 3.2 Quality Standards
- Upload high-quality photos and videos
- Write clear, professional descriptions
- Use appropriate language and grammar
- Keep content relevant to the entertainment industry

### 3.3 Original Content
- Only upload content you have the right to share
- Do not plagiarize or misrepresent others' work as your own
- Credit collaborators appropriately
- Respect intellectual property rights

---

## 4. Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on the Platform:

### 4.1 Illegal Content
- Content that violates any applicable law
- Fraudulent schemes or scams
- Content related to illegal drugs or weapons
- Stolen content or content obtained through illegal means

### 4.2 Harmful Content
- Content that promotes self-harm or suicide
- Content that glorifies violence or terrorism
- Graphic or disturbing content without appropriate warnings
- Content that encourages dangerous or illegal behavior

### 4.3 Sexually Explicit Content
- Nudity or sexually explicit material (except where properly labeled and age-restricted in casting calls with full disclosure)
- Sexual solicitation or escort services
- Non-consensual intimate imagery (revenge porn)
- Content sexualizing minors

### 4.4 Deceptive Content
- Catfishing or impersonation
- Deepfakes used to deceive (excluding properly labeled AI-generated content)
- Fake casting calls or fraudulent opportunities
- Phishing attempts or malware

### 4.5 Spam and Manipulation
- Unsolicited commercial messages
- Fake accounts or bot activity
- Manipulation of ratings, reviews, or search results
- Coordinated inauthentic behavior

---

## 5. Reporting Violations

### 5.1 How to Report
If you encounter content or behavior that violates these Guidelines, report it using:
- The **Report** button on profiles, casting calls, or messages
- Email: report@bigstarz.com
- In-app reporting tools

### 5.2 What to Include
When reporting, please provide:
- Description of the violation
- Link to the content or user profile
- Screenshots or evidence (if applicable)
- Your contact information (for follow-up)

### 5.3 Anonymous Reporting
You may report violations anonymously, but providing contact information helps us investigate and may be necessary for follow-up.

### 5.4 False Reporting
Submitting false reports is a violation of these Guidelines and may result in account action.

## 6. Enforcement

### 6.1 Violation Levels
Violations are categorized by severity:

| Level | Description | Examples |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Minor** | Unintentional, first-time issues | Minor profile inaccuracies, off-topic content |
| **Moderate** | Deliberate but not harmful | Spam, misleading casting details |
| **Serious** | Harmful or repeated violations | Harassment, discrimination, fake profiles |
| **Severe** | Dangerous or illegal | Threats, CSAM, fraud, hacking |

### 6.2 Enforcement Actions
Depending on the severity and history of violations:

| Action | Applies To |
|--------|-----------|
| **Warning** | Minor, first-time violations |
| **Content Removal** | Content that violates Guidelines |
| **Temporary Suspension** | Moderate violations, repeat minor violations |
| **Permanent Ban** | Serious violations, repeat moderate violations, severe violations |
| **Legal Action** | Severe violations, illegal activity |

### 6.3 Account History
We consider your account history when determining enforcement actions:
- First-time offenders may receive warnings or lighter penalties
- Repeat offenders face progressively stricter penalties
- Severe violations may result in immediate permanent bans regardless of history

---

## 7. Appeals

### 7.1 Right to Appeal
If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error, you may appeal:

### 7.2 Appeal Process
1. Submit an appeal within 14 days of the enforcement action
2. Email: appeals@bigstarz.com with subject line "Appeal — [Your Username]"
3. Include:
   - Your account email
   - Description of the action taken
   - Explanation of why you believe it was in error
   - Any supporting evidence

### 7.3 Appeal Review
- Appeals are reviewed by a team separate from the original decision
- Response time: 7-14 business days
- Decisions are final but may be revisited if new evidence emerges

## 8. Industry-Specific Guidelines

### 8.1 For Talent
- Maintain accurate and up-to-date profiles
- Respond professionally to casting directors
- Honor commitments (auditions, callbacks)
- Report inappropriate casting calls

### 8.2 For Casting Directors
- Post accurate, complete casting calls
- Respond to applications promptly
- Treat all talent with respect and fairness
- Comply with labor laws and union rules
- Provide safe working environments

### 8.3 For Creators
- Ensure AI-generated content is properly labeled
- Respect others' likeness rights
- Do not use AI to create deceptive content
- Follow content licensing rules
