## 11. Revocation

To revoke this consent, contact:
- **Email:** privacy@bigstarz.com
- **Subject Line:** "Guardian Consent Revocation — [Minor's Name]"
- Include: Guardian name, minor name, account email, reason for revocation

Upon revocation:
- The minor's account will be suspended or deleted
- All public content will be removed
- AI-generated content will be queued for deletion
- Outstanding issues will be resolved within 30 days
