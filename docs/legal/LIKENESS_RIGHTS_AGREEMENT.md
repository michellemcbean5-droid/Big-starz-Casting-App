# Likeness Rights Agreement

**Big Starz Casting Platform**

**Effective Date:** Upon Signature

---

This Likeness Rights Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between you ("Talent," "you," or "your") and Big Starz Inc. ("Platform," "we," "us," or "our"). By signing this Agreement, you grant the Platform certain rights to use your likeness, image, voice, and persona as described below.

---

## 1. Grant of Likeness Rights

### 1.1 License Grant
You hereby grant the Platform a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable, and transferable license to use, reproduce, distribute, prepare derivative works of, display, and perform your likeness, image, voice, name, and persona (collectively, "Likeness") in connection with the Services.

### 1.2 Purpose
The license granted herein is for the following purposes:
- Creating and displaying your profile on the Platform
- Facilitating casting calls and applications
- Generating AI content through the Platform's AI Studio
- Creating and maintaining digital twins
- Promoting the Platform and its Services
- Training and improving AI models (with your separate consent)

---

## 2. Scope of Use

### 2.1 Permitted Uses
The Platform may use your Likeness for:
- **Profile Display:** Displaying your photos, videos, and biographical information on your public profile
- **Casting Facilitation:** Sharing your profile with casting directors who post relevant casting calls
- **AI Content Generation:** Using your Likeness as input for AI-generated scenes, reels, music videos, and other content
- **Digital Twin Creation:** Creating and maintaining a digital representation of your Likeness
- **Platform Promotion:** Featuring your success stories, testimonials, and portfolio highlights in Platform marketing materials
- **Analytics:** Analyzing anonymized likeness data to improve matching algorithms

### 2.2 Prohibited Uses
The Platform will NOT use your Likeness for:
- Pornographic, obscene, or defamatory content
- Content that promotes hate speech, violence, or discrimination
- Political advertising or endorsements without your explicit consent
- Content that misrepresents your identity, views, or affiliations
- Transfer to third parties outside the scope of Platform Services

### 2.3 Attribution
When your Likeness is used in promotional materials, we will make reasonable efforts to credit you by name or stage name, unless you opt out of such attribution.

---

## 3. AI and Digital Twin Rights

### 3.1 Digital Twin Creation
You authorize the Platform to create a digital twin using:
- Photographs you upload (minimum 4 high-quality images)
- Video clips you provide
- Voice samples (if applicable)

### 3.2 Digital Twin Usage
Your digital twin may be used for:
- Generating AI scenes and auditions
- Creating virtual casting materials
- Testing AI model improvements
- Generating content for your portfolio

### 3.3 AI-Generated Content Ownership
AI-generated content that incorporates your digital twin is jointly owned as follows:
- You retain ownership of the underlying Likeness
- The Platform retains ownership of the AI generation technology and methodology
- Generated content may be used by both parties as specified in this Agreement and the [AI Consent Agreement](AI_CONSENT_AGREEMENT.md)

### 3.4 Watermarking
All AI-generated content incorporating your Likeness will be watermarked to indicate AI generation. You may not remove or alter these watermarks.

---

## 4. Compensation

### 4.1 No Direct Compensation for Basic Use
The license granted for basic Platform use (profile display, casting facilitation) is provided without direct monetary compensation as it is integral to the Services.

### 4.2 Revenue Share for AI Content
For Gold-tier creators, revenue generated from AI content incorporating your Likeness is subject to the revenue share terms in the [Talent Contract](TALENT_CONTRACT.md).

### 4.3 Promotional Use Compensation
If your Likeness is used in significant promotional campaigns beyond standard Platform features, you will be notified and may negotiate additional compensation.

---

## 5. Term and Termination

### 5.1 Term
This Agreement is effective as of the date you sign it and continues until terminated.

### 5.2 Termination by You
You may terminate this Agreement at any time by:
- Deleting your account
- Submitting a written termination request to legal@bigstarz.com
- Revoking consent through the Platform's privacy settings

### 5.3 Termination by Platform
We may terminate this Agreement if you violate the Terms of Service or if required by law.

### 5.4 Effect of Termination
Upon termination:
- The Platform will cease using your Likeness for new content
- Your profile will be removed from public view
- Existing AI-generated content may remain on the Platform per the survival clause below
- Your digital twin will be scheduled for deletion within 30 days

### 5.5 Survival
Sections related to AI-generated content created prior to termination survive termination, as such content cannot be practically retracted from distribution.
