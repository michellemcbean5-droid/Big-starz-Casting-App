## 1. Introduction

Big Starz Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Big Starz Casting Platform ("Services").

This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California residents, and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for users under 13.

By using our Services, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

### 6.1 Retention Periods
- **Account information:** Retained until account deletion, plus 7 years for legal compliance
- **Content:** Retained per your instructions and legal requirements
- **Payment records:** Retained for 7 years (tax compliance)
- **Log data:** Retained for 2 years
- **Deleted accounts:** Personal data anonymized within 30 days, with certain records retained for legal compliance

### 6.2 Account Deletion
When you delete your account:
- Your profile is immediately removed from public view
- Personal data is anonymized within 30 days
- Legal records (contracts, payments) are retained as required
- AI-generated content may be retained per the AI Consent Agreement

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EEA Users)
- **Right to Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your data
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to certain types of processing
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent for AI processing at any time

### 7.2 CCPA Rights (California Residents)
- **Right to Know:** Request disclosure of personal information collected
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt out of sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

### 7.3 COPPA Rights (Parents/Guardians)
Parents and guardians have the right to:
- Review personal information collected from their child
- Request deletion of their child's personal information
- Refuse further collection or use of their child's information
- Provide consent for collection and use of their child's information

### 7.4 Exercising Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@bigstarz.com or use the privacy controls in your account settings. We will respond to requests within 30 days.

### 7.5 Data Export
You can request a complete export of your data through the "Download My Data" feature in your account settings. The export includes:
- Profile information
- Content you've uploaded
- Application history
- Payment records
- Contract and consent records

## 8. Children's Privacy

### 8.1 Age Requirements
Our Services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

### 8.2 Guardian Consent
Users between 13 and 18 must have a parent or legal guardian complete the [Guardian Consent Form](GUARDIAN_CONSENT_FORM.md). The guardian:
- Must create and manage the account on behalf of the minor
- Is responsible for all activity on the account
- Can revoke consent and delete the account at any time

### 8.3 Information from Minors
We collect only the information necessary for the minor to use the Services, and we do not share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

### 8.4 Notice to Parents
If you believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at privacy@bigstarz.com. We will take steps to delete the information and terminate the account.
