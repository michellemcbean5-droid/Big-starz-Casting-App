# Refund Policy

**Big Starz Casting Platform**

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

---

## 1. Subscription Refund Terms

### 1.1 General Policy
All subscription purchases on the Big Starz Platform are subject to this Refund Policy. By subscribing to any paid plan, you agree to these terms.

### 1.2 Eligibility for Refunds
You may be eligible for a refund under the following conditions:

**Full Refunds:**
- Request made within 14 days of initial subscription purchase
- You have not used any paid features (AI generations, premium tools)
- The subscription was purchased in error
- Technical issues prevented access to paid features (verified by support)

**Partial (Prorated) Refunds:**
- Cancellation after the 14-day window
- Upgrade to a higher tier within the same billing cycle
- Account suspension by Platform for reasons other than Terms violations

### 1.3 Non-Refundable Scenarios
Refunds will NOT be provided in the following situations:
- You have used paid features during the subscription period
- The refund request is made after 30 days from purchase
- Your account was suspended or terminated due to Terms of Service violations
- You received a promotional or discounted subscription
- You failed to cancel before the next billing cycle (auto-renewal)
- Dissatisfaction with casting outcomes or AI generation quality
- Change of mind after using the Services

## 2. Prorated Refunds

### 2.1 Calculation Method
Prorated refunds are calculated as follows:
```
Refund Amount = (Total Subscription Cost / Days in Billing Period) × Remaining Days
```

### 2.2 Deductions
The following may be deducted from prorated refunds:
- Processing fees (3% of original transaction)
- Value of used AI generation credits (at the per-credit rate of your tier)
- Value of premium features used during the subscription period

### 2.3 Example
If you purchased a Silver subscription ($29.99/month) and cancel after 10 days:
- Daily rate: $29.99 / 30 = $1.00
- Remaining days: 20
- Prorated amount: $20.00
- Less processing fee (3%): $0.60
- Less 20 AI credits used (at $0.30/credit): $6.00
- **Final refund: $13.40**

## 3. How to Request a Refund

### 3.1 Process
To request a refund:
1. Log in to your Big Starz account
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Request Refund**
3. Fill out the refund request form with:
   - Reason for refund
   - Date of purchase
   - Subscription tier
   - Any relevant details
4. Submit the request

### 3.2 Alternative Method
You may also request a refund by emailing billing@bigstarz.com with:
- Your account email address
- Order/transaction ID
- Reason for refund request
- Any supporting documentation

---

## 4. Processing Time

### 4.1 Review Period
Refund requests are reviewed within 3-5 business days. You will receive an email notification of the decision.

### 4.2 Processing Time
If approved:
- **Credit/Debit Cards:** 5-10 business days to appear on your statement
- **PayPal:** 3-5 business days
- **Bank Transfer:** 7-14 business days

The exact timing depends on your payment provider and bank.

### 4.3 Denied Requests
If your refund request is denied, you will receive an explanation and may appeal the decision within 14 days by contacting billing@bigstarz.com.

## 5. Special Circumstances

### 5.1 Annual Subscriptions
Annual subscriptions are eligible for the same refund terms, calculated on a prorated basis from the annual purchase date.

### 5.2 Promotional Subscriptions
Subscriptions obtained through promotions, giveaways, or master access codes are not eligible for refunds.

### 5.3 Gift Subscriptions
Gift subscriptions are non-refundable but may be transferred to another user with Platform approval.

### 5.4 Force Majeure
In the event of Platform-wide service outages or discontinuation, all active subscribers will receive a full prorated refund regardless of usage.
