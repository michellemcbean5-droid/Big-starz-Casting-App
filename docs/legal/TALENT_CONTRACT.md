# Talent Contract

**Big Starz Casting Platform — Talent Engagement Agreement**

**Effective Date:** Upon Signature

---

This Talent Contract ("Contract") is entered into between you ("Talent") and Big Starz Inc. ("Platform"). This Contract governs your engagement with casting opportunities and monetization features on the Platform.

---

## 1. Engagement Terms

### 1.1 Platform Membership
As a Talent member of the Platform, you are granted:
- Access to casting call listings and application system
- Profile creation and management tools
- AI Studio access (subject to subscription tier)
- Communication tools for interacting with casting directors

### 1.2 Non-Exclusive Engagement
Your engagement with the Platform is non-exclusive. You are free to:
- Pursue opportunities outside the Platform
- Maintain profiles on other casting platforms
- Work with casting directors directly

### 1.3 Platform Commission
For casting opportunities facilitated through the Platform that result in paid engagement, the Platform charges a commission as follows:
- Standard: 10% of gross compensation
- Gold-tier members: 5% of gross compensation
- Direct bookings (no Platform facilitation): 0% commission

---

## 2. Compensation

### 2.1 Casting Opportunity Compensation
All compensation for casting opportunities is negotiated directly between Talent and Casting Director. The Platform does not set or guarantee compensation rates.

### 2.2 AI Content Revenue Share
Gold-tier Talent are eligible for revenue sharing on AI-generated content:
- **Rate:** 15% of net revenue from AI content incorporating your likeness
- **Payment:** Monthly, within 30 days of month-end
- **Minimum Payout:** $50.00
- **Tracking:** Detailed analytics available in Creator Dashboard

### 2.3 Referral Bonuses
Talent may earn referral bonuses for bringing new users to the Platform:
- **Talent referral:** $10 per verified new Talent
- **Director referral:** $25 per verified new Casting Director
- **Paid to your earnings balance**

### 2.4 Payment Terms
- Payments are processed through Stripe Connect
- US-based Talent: ACH transfer (5-7 business days)
- International Talent: PayPal or wire transfer
- Tax documentation (W-9 or W-8BEN) required for all payouts

---

## 3. Exclusivity

### 3.1 No Exclusivity Requirement
The Platform does not require exclusive representation. You retain full freedom to:
- Accept representation from agents or managers
- Join other casting platforms
- Pursue opportunities through any channel

### 3.2 Exclusivity Option
If you choose to grant the Platform exclusive representation for specific projects or territories, such arrangement must be documented in a separate written agreement.

---

## 4. Term

### 4.1 Initial Term
This Contract begins on the date of signature and continues for a period of one (1) year.

### 4.2 Renewal
The Contract automatically renews for successive one-year periods unless terminated by either party.

### 4.3 Early Termination
Either party may terminate this Contract with 30 days' written notice. No penalty applies for early termination.

---

## 5. Termination

### 5.1 Termination by Talent
You may terminate this Contract at any time by:
- Deleting your account
- Sending written notice to talent-relations@bigstarz.com
- Ceasing use of the Platform

### 5.2 Termination by Platform
The Platform may terminate this Contract if you:
- Violate the Terms of Service
- Violate the Community Guidelines
- Engage in fraudulent activity
- Fail to maintain accurate profile information

### 5.3 Post-Termination
Upon termination:
- Outstanding earnings will be paid within 30 days
- Your profile will be removed from public view
- Active applications may be withdrawn or archived
- AI-generated content per the AI Consent Agreement remains subject to those terms

---

## 6. Confidentiality

### 6.1 Platform Confidentiality
You agree to maintain the confidentiality of:
- Casting call details marked as confidential
- Communications with casting directors
- Proprietary Platform features and algorithms
- Other users' private information

### 6.2 Talent Confidentiality
The Platform agrees to maintain the confidentiality of:
- Your contact information (shared only with relevant casting directors)
- Your financial information
- Private communications
- Unpublished portfolio materials

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

### 7.1 Your Content
You retain ownership of all content you upload to the Platform, subject to the licenses granted in the Terms of Service and Likeness Rights Agreement.

### 7.2 AI-Generated Content
AI-generated content is governed by the [AI Consent Agreement](AI_CONSENT_AGREEMENT.md) and [Likeness Rights Agreement](LIKENESS_RIGHTS_AGREEMENT.md).

### 7.3 Platform IP
You acknowledge that the Platform's technology, algorithms, and branding are the exclusive property of Big Starz Inc.

---

## 8. Dispute Resolution

### 8.1 Negotiation
Parties agree to first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation.

### 8.2 Mediation
If negotiation fails, parties agree to non-binding mediation before pursuing arbitration.

### 8.3 Arbitration
Unresolved disputes shall be settled by binding arbitration in Los Angeles, California, per the Terms of Service.
